Type 01

Educational Evaluation

Examines cognitive and academic functioning and may help inform IEP, 504, and school accommodations. It does not necessarily result in a clinical diagnosis.

Swipe →
Type 02

Psychological Evaluation

Examines emotional, behavioral, personality, and functional concerns to support diagnosis and treatment planning.

Swipe →
Type 03

Neuropsychological Evaluation

Uses specialized knowledge of brain–behavior relationships to examine areas such as attention, memory, language, executive functioning, visual-spatial skills, motor skills, and intelligence.

Swipe →
Type 04

Neurodevelopmental Evaluation

Examines developmental patterns and may help clarify concerns such as autism, ADHD, developmental delays, and adaptive functioning.
